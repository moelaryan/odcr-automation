Serverless capacity automation on Azure

SCR — Scheduled Capacity Reservation

Provisions guaranteed GPU capacity (ODCRs) across regions before the daily exam window, retries through shortages, then releases it at night to control cost.
⏰
Schedule
Timer fires every 5 min, 5–8am ET. ~3-hour retry runway.

Self-healing retry

No secrets

💸
Cost-aware
Capacity held only during the window; serverless scales to zero.
